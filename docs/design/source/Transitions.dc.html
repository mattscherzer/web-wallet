Transitions

Six M3 patterns. Pick by the relationship between the two states: the stronger the relationship, the more is shared. Every transition is skipped, not shortened, when reduced motion is on: the new state simply appears.

Container transform

One container grows into the next view. Strongest link between two things.

In Treasury
Record button to the Record menu, a history row to its Entry, the wallet button to the switcher sheet.

Motion
400 ms · emphasized (0.2, 0, 0, 1) · container, icon and label persist

Forward and backward

Slide plus fade between consecutive levels of hierarchy.

In Treasury
History to Entry, More to Categories or People, Entry to Edit. Back reverses it.

Motion
300 ms · slide 30% of the width with a fade · emphasized

Lateral

Peers slide as one group with no fade, hinting that they can be swiped.

In Treasury
Money in and Money out on Categories, the Record type switch, report periods.

Motion
300 ms · whole group moves together · emphasized

Top level

Fade through between unrelated destinations. No shared element.

In Treasury
Overview, History, Reports and More in the navigation bar.

Motion
90 ms out, 210 ms in · standard · no movement

Enter and exit

Expand away from the edge a component belongs to. No scale.

In Treasury
Snackbars rise from the bottom, banners drop from the top, sheets slide up, dialogs fade in place.

Motion
250 ms in, 200 ms out · emphasized decelerate / accelerate

Skeleton loader

A subtle pulse that sweeps from the top left to the bottom right, then the content fades in on top.

In Treasury
First load of Overview, History and Entry. The skeleton mirrors the real layout so nothing jumps. See the two Loading screens.

Motion
Pulse 1.6 s, 0.9 s sweep delay · fade in 200 ms · standard
