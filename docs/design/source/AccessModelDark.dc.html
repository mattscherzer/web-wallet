Who can do what

One owner with full access, and staff who record transactions, like a till. Staff have no account: the owner gives them a username and a PIN. Every entry is saved under the name of whoever made it.

How people get in

Which screen appears, and when.

  1. Opening the wallet link shows the books, view only.
  2. Staff tap Unlock, type their username and enter their PIN once. The session locks after 15 minutes without activity.
  3. The owner signs in with email and password. Forgot it? A reset link goes to the email.
  4. Owner-only actions ask for the password again, then stay open for 5 minutes.
  5. If staff tap an owner-only action, a sheet says it’s for the owner and offers owner sign-in.
  6. After 5 wrong PINs the username pauses for 15 minutes. The owner can reset the PIN.

Proposals to confirm

Chosen as sensible defaults, not researched.

  1. 15 minute inactivity lock.
  2. 5 minute owner confirmation window.
  3. 6-digit PIN, 5 tries, 15 minute pause.
  4. Owner passwords: 10 characters or more.
  5. Staff can remove entries, but only the owner can restore them.
