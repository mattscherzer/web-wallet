Loading indicator

M3 Expressive. A morphing shape for a wait under 5 s with no known length. Replaces every indeterminate spinner. Uncontained in banners and buttons; contained where the background has no contrast, e.g. pull to refresh.

48 dp box, 38 dp shape. Never turns into a progress bar.

Progress indicator

When the amount is known: sync n of m, export, import. Wavy for active work, flat for quiet ones. Track is secondary container, stop dot at the end, inset 4 dp from the edge. Use one configuration for every instance of a process.

Skeleton loader

Shapes that hint at where content will land and keep the layout still. A subtle pulse sweeps from the top left to the bottom right; the content then fades in on top within 200 ms.

Full-page states

Whole-screen messages use the 96 px shape icon, a plain headline, one sentence of what happened and what is safe, and at most three actions. See the screens beside this board for each case.

  • Loading: skeleton of the screen
  • Empty: no entries yet, no results, nothing in period
  • Error: history failed, record failed
  • Access: wallet unavailable, link expired
