How people get in

Sign in is the first screen on a new phone. Nothing loads before that.

  1. First time on a phone: staff type their username and PIN, owners use email and password. The phone stays signed in until someone signs out.
  2. After 30 minutes without use, or from the lock button, the phone locks. Balances, history and reports stay visible; nothing can be changed.
  3. To change something, unlock: staff enter their PIN, owners their password. Tapping an action while locked asks too.
  4. Owner-only actions ask for the password again, then stay open for 15 minutes.
  5. After 5 wrong PINs a username pauses for 30 seconds, doubling up to an hour. The owner can reset the PIN.
  6. Lost owner password: reset by email through the sign-in provider. There is no screen for it yet.
